Groundwind and the apps you already carry

Use it beside onX and Windy.

Most of us already have onX or iHunter for the land and Windy for the weather, and they cover the whole map. Groundwind is your active hunt partner on the spot you're hunting. It helps you make better decisions when it counts, with the wind at head height on your ground and where your scent goes on it. Here's what each app does, so you can see where it fits.

Checked 9 October 2026 against each app's own pages. Apps change every season, so if one has, tell us at hello@groundwind.app.

Every app starts from a forecast.

So does Groundwind. A forecast is worked out for 10 m up over open ground, and the other apps here show it to you at a pin, from a weather model or the nearest station. Groundwind takes the 2.5 km HD forecast and solves it over the 1 m LiDAR ground and every stand of trees, down to head height, then draws your scent on that. How the wind model works.

onX Hunt

onX Maps, Montana · Premium US$34.99, Elite US$99.99 a year

  • Keep it forWho owns the land across the US, Crown land and parcels in Canada, polished offline maps, and the Elite terrain tools.
  • Groundwind addsThe wind at head height on your ground, a scent cone on it, thermals, and the wind checks that correct it.
What we checked
  • onX's weather comes from Xweather. The wind on a waypoint is from the nearest weather station, and Optimal Wind colours each waypoint green, yellow or red against the winds you've said suit it. The Wind Calendar gives seven days at sunrise, midday and sunset (onX help; onX help).
  • onX says its forecasts account for elevation, slope aspect and water (onX). We found no wind drawn over the ground or under the trees, and no scent cone.
  • The weather needs a connection. On a phone plan with satellite data, T-Mobile's in the US or Rogers' in Canada, it comes in by satellite (onX).
  • In Canada the Land Data layer shows Crown land, provincial forests and parks, and private parcels where they're available, without owners' names (onX help).
  • In the Canadian App Store, Premium covers one state or Canada for CA$34.99 a year (App Store). A viewshed tool comes with Elite, on the desktop web map (onX).

HuntStand

GSM Outdoors · free, Pro US$34.99, Ultimate US$99.99 a year

  • Keep it forProperty lines in the US and most of Canada for little money, offline maps, your stands and cameras, and a free scent cone for planning the sit.
  • Groundwind addsA cone that bends with the ridge, the trees and the evening drainage, the whole party's scent at once, and your own wind checks.
What we checked
  • HuntZone is free. Tap a spot and it shows the forecast wind hour by hour, up to 72 hours ahead, with a scent cone projected downwind (App Store; HuntStand's guide). We found nothing saying the wind or the cone bends with the ground or the trees, and HuntStand doesn't name its weather model.
  • Pro adds property lines, with owners' names across the US, and boundaries and Crown land in most of Canada. A Tree Cover layer and LiDAR terrain maps came in 2025 (HuntStand help).
  • Its help pages have iPhone users tap Download Weather each day to keep the forecast for when there's no signal (HuntStand help, 2023).
  • Pro is US$34.99 a year on HuntStand's site and US$29.99 in the App Store listing (HuntStand).
  • ScoutLook, the app with the ScentCone, merged into HuntStand in 2019 and isn't sold any more (HuntStand).

HuntWise

Sportsman Tracker · free, Pro US$59.99, Elite US$119.99 a year

  • Keep it forHuntCast's hour-by-hour hunt scores, your stands ranked by the day's wind up to 15 days out, and US landowner details.
  • Groundwind addsThe wind where you'll actually sit, under the trees and in the evening drainage, a cone that follows it, and the wind with no signal.
What we checked
  • WindCast, in Pro, ranks the stands you've saved by the forecast wind, hour by hour up to 15 days out, and draws a wind cone (HuntWise; WindCast guide). HuntWise's own 2026 review lists "no micro-thermal modeling yet" (HuntWise).
  • Its guide says not to expect WindCast without a connection (WindCast guide).
  • In Canada it has no property lines, landowner details or road layers (HuntWise).
  • HuntCast scores each hour from 1 to 10 from the weather, the moon and movement history (HuntWise).

iHunter

Inside Outside Studios, Canada · free, Pro CA$14.99 a province a year, Ontario CA$44.99

  • Keep it forYour province's regulations and WMUs, and Crown and private land layers that work offline, at a price that's hard to beat.
  • Groundwind addsEverything about the wind and your scent, and a forecast that works with no bars.
What we checked
  • iHunter shows the weather and wind for a point or a waypoint, from local weather reports, while you have signal (iHunter; App Store).
  • Ontario's Pro adds 14 land layers, General Use Crown Land and private land among them (iHunter).
  • It covers BC, Alberta, Saskatchewan, Manitoba, Ontario, Québec, New Brunswick, Nova Scotia, Newfoundland and Labrador, and the Yukon (App Store).

Windy

Windy.com, Czech Republic · free, Premium US$24.99 (CA$34.99) a year

  • Keep it forThe big picture before you pick a day. A dozen weather models side by side, fronts, rain and radar across the region.
  • Groundwind addsThe same HD forecast brought down to head height on your ground, your scent on it, and all of it with no signal.
What we checked
  • Windy offers HRDPS, the 2.5 km forecast Groundwind starts from, beside ECMWF at 9 km, GFS at 22 km and about a dozen more. All the models are free (Windy; Windy community).
  • Its wind is 10 m up, with levels above that, on each model's own grid. It doesn't adjust for a hillside or a stand of trees, and doesn't say it does (Windy community).
  • Its Thermals layer is the rising air glider pilots fly, not the air sliding down a slope at dusk.
  • A stations layer shows weather stations, personal ones too, with how far each model's wind was off there.
  • There's no offline mode. Work on one stopped in 2024 (Windy community).
  • Premium adds hourly steps, a 15-day outlook and more updates a day (Windy).

Where they're ahead

  • Who owns the land. Groundwind has no property lines or Crown land layers. onX, HuntStand and iHunter all have them, in Canada too.
  • Everywhere at once. They cover whole states and provinces the day you install them. Groundwind goes deep on the spot you hunt instead, with a 10 × 10 km block built around the place you ask for.
  • The app stores. They're in the App Store and Google Play. Groundwind runs in your phone's browser for now, and goes on your home screen from there.
  • Years in the field. These apps have been in hunters' pockets for years. Groundwind is a beta with one season of wind checks behind it.

How right is anyone's wind?

No hunting app on this page, Groundwind included, publishes how often its wind matches what hunters feel at the stand. Windy comes closest. Each weather station on its map shows how far each model's wind was off there. Groundwind's hunt log keeps that score for you, check by check, beside what the forecast said, so you can judge it on your own ground.
