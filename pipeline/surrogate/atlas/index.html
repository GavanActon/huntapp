What this is

Each site is a 15 × 15 km box of 30 m terrain. HD is WindNinja's momentum solve for a wind from the chosen direction (neutral air, terrain only, 10 m above the ground). SD is the surrogate net's prediction for the same terrain, the wind an SD area gets.

Held out means the net never saw that site, so its SD is what a new area would get. In-sample means the net trained on that site's own WindNinja runs, so the two will look closer there than they would anywhere new.

Streak speed follows the forecast wind with Now, else the solve's own 22 km/h. Neither model has stability: no night drainage, no rotors.
