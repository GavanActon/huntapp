## Extracted facts
- Moose, columns: antlered early-season special licence | antlered single or late-season special licence | outfitter archery | outfitter rut | outfitter rifle | three deferral columns | antlerless resident special licence | calf resident special licence.
  - WMU 324: early —; single/late **3**; outfitter archery 4; rifle 6; deferrals 2 and 2; antlerless —; calf —.
  - WMU 326: early **6**; single/late **4**; outfitter archery 4, rut 1, rifle 2; deferrals 2 and 1; antlerless —; calf —.
- Elk: WMU 324 antlered = "General season"; outfitter archery 2, rifle 2, rifle deferrals 2; antlerless resident special licence **1 in each of seasons I–IV** (4 in all). WMU 326 antlered = "General season"; outfitter archery 2, rifle 4; no antlerless licences.
- White-tailed deer: antlered and antlerless = "General season" in both 324 and 326; outfitter allocations 324 archery 5, rifle 13 (+10 and 12 deferrals); 326 archery 4, rifle 11.
- Mule deer: antlered special licence **5** in 324 and **5** in 326; landowner 1 each; outfitter 324 archery 4, rifle 11 (+2, 5); 326 rifle 22 (+1); no antlerless.
- The COVID "deferrals eligible for use" are 2020–21 outfitter allocations usable until the 2026–27 season (note on each report).

## What it means for Clearwater
- [I] Moose here is a draw hunt with about 13 resident antlered licences across both WMUs (3 + 6 + 4). The moose target should read "permit only" (regs.ts status `draw`), not plainly hunted.
- [I] Elk bulls are a general season, but the only antlerless elk is the 324 archery season plus 4 late-season draw licences: managers are not trying to reduce elk here.
- [I] White-tailed deer of either sex on a general licence: deer is the open, general hunt in this box.
