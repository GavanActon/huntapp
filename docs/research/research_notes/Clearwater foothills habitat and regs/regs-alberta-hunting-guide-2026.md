# Alberta Guide to Hunting Regulations 2026: what applies in WMUs 324 and 326

- **Source:** Alberta Forestry and Parks / Sports Scene Publications, *2026 Alberta Guide to Hunting Regulations* (96 pp., PDF created 31 July 2026, last modified 14 Sept 2026), https://www.albertaregulations.ca/2026-Alberta-Hunting-Regulations.pdf; the same tables online: https://www.albertaregulations.ca/huntingregs/wmu300.html (Foothills big game), https://www.albertaregulations.ca/huntingregs/bear-cougar-seasons.html, https://www.albertaregulations.ca/huntingregs/bird-seasons.html, https://www.albertaregulations.ca/huntingregs/gameregs.html, https://www.albertaregulations.ca/huntingregs/genregs.html, https://www.albertaregulations.ca/huntingregs/changes.html
- **Read:** 2026-10-17 (full text; the Foothills table, bear table and game bird table were also checked against the rendered PDF pages 52, 53, 58 and 63, which match the HTML)
- **Evidence:** [G] regulation summary (not the law; the guide says the online version has precedence over print)
- **Where and when:** Alberta, licence year 2026–27; WMU 324 Prairie Creek and WMU 326 Baseline (Foothills, 300 series)

## Extracted facts

Legend, as printed (p. 52): "Archery-only seasons are those where only a bow and arrow may be used to hunt. General seasons are those where either a firearm, crossbow or a bow and arrow may be used." "■ Indicates seasons that apply only to hunters with applicable Special Licences." Month letters: A Aug, S Sept, O Oct, N Nov, D Dec, J Jan, F Feb.

- "Three-point elk – A male elk bearing an antler that is composed of a main beam from which project not fewer than two tines, each of which is at least 7.6 cm (3 in.) in length. Note: The tip of the main beam must be at least 7.6 cm (3 in.) from the base of the last tine counted." (gameregs.html)
- "Antlered – A white-tailed deer, moose or elk having an antler exceeding 10.2 cm (4 in.) in length." (gameregs.html; changes.html adds mule deer.)
- General mule deer licence: "valid during the archery-only season, which precedes the general season. It is valid during the general season in the following WMUs: 352, 353, 355, 440-446, 512-519, 528-534, 536, 539-542 and 841. This licence is not valid during a season in which a special licence is required." (p. 68) So in 326 a general mule deer licence covers antlered bucks by bow 25 Aug–16 Sept only.
- A drawn bowhunter "may, if an open early archery season is offered, hunt under the authority of that licence during the archery season but only in the WMU specified on the licence" (gameregs.html, Bowhunting). Crossbows may not be used in archery-only seasons (gameregs.html).
- "Moose (archery)" exists as a general licence (resident licence table, p. 21), but every moose season in 324 and 326 carries ■, so it does not apply there.
- Changes for 2026 (changes.html): "Late elk seasons have been split into separate draw options in the following WMUs (… 320-324 …)"; partner licences for elk special licences introduced.

Black bear (p. 58):
- WMU 326: fall 2026 archery-only 25–31 Aug, general 1 Sept–30 Nov; spring 2027 general 1 Apr–15 June ("requires a new year licence").
- WMU 324: fall 2026 general 1 Sept–30 Nov; spring 2027 1 Apr–15 June.
- Baiting permitted in 322, 330–338 … "and portions of WMUs 320, 324, 357, 507, 521, 526 and 528" (check local maps); 326 is not on the list. Baiting is prohibited within 1.6 km of occupied dwellings and of provincial parks and recreation areas; during November only meat may be used.
- Supplemental (second) black bear licences valid in 224, 250, 258, 260, 320–360, 429, 445, 500–544 and 841.
- Unlawful to hunt a black bear under one year old or a female with a cub under one year.

Cougar (bear-cougar-seasons.html): WMUs 300–360 are open 1 Dec 2026–31 Mar 2027 only; there is a male and a female quota and hunters "must call 1-800-661-3729 (toll free) prior to hunting each day to determine if the season remains open." The 25 Aug–30 Nov resident, no-dog season does not include 324 or 326.

Wolf and coyote (gameregs.html, Other Species):
- "Timber Wolf – A resident may, without a licence, hunt (but not trap) timber wolf from the opening of any big game season in a particular WMU to May 31, 2027, or until June 15, 2027 in WMUs where black bear seasons are open until June 15, 2027." 324 and 326 have black bear to 15 June, so wolf runs from the first big game opener (25 Aug in 326, 1 Sept in 324) to 15 June 2027.
- Coyote: all year on private land and White Area public land with right of access; on Green Area public land from the opening of a big game season in the WMU to 31 May (printed "May 31, 2026", evidently 2027) or 15 June where spring bear runs to then.
- Wolf/coyote baiting on public land only 1 Dec–31 Mar or during an open black bear baiting season.

Game birds (p. 63): ruffed grouse, spruce grouse, blue grouse and ptarmigan each 5 a day, 15 in possession, open 1 Sept–15 Jan in WMUs 102–402 (ptarmigan and blue grouse rows: 300–402). Sharp-tailed grouse 1–31 Oct only in "102-246, 252-256, 300-314, 334, 400-402, 518-520, 524, 525, 528-542, 936": 324 and 326 are not included, so no sharp-tailed season there. Gray partridge 1 Sept–15 Jan; male pheasant 1 Sept–15 Jan (314–402). Ducks and geese 1 Sept–16 Dec (316–544).

General rules that bear on the map (genregs.html):
- Hours: unlawful to "Hunt any wildlife or discharge a firearm between one-half hour after sunset and one-half hour before sunrise."
- Roads: unlawful to "Discharge a firearm from or cause a projectile from a firearm to pass along or across: a) a provincial highway … b) a road that is paved, oiled, graded or regularly maintained", unless the road is held under a Public Lands Act disposition or Surface Rights order, or the person is shooting game birds with a shotgun. "if there is no identifiable ditch or fence … the roadway extends 20 feet from the edge of the traveled portion." The same applies to arrows at big game.
- Buildings: no discharge within 183 m (200 yards) of an occupied building without the occupant's consent.
- Loaded firearm in or on a vehicle (moving or not) prohibited.
- Sunday big game hunting is prohibited only "in WMUs 102-160" and "in WMUs 624, 728, 730 and 936": Sundays are open in 324 and 326.
- Private and occupied land: permission to enter AND express consent to hunt are both required (Wildlife Act s. 38, Petty Trespass Act); "Public land under agricultural or grazing lease" needs the leaseholder's permission.
- OHVs: "may be limited or prohibited within … Public Land Use Zones"; the Kiska/Willson PLUZ ("Designated trails only with some seasonal restrictions") lists WMU 326 among its WMUs, but the PLUZ lies 25 km west of the box (measured; see place-land-status.md). The rule against carrying a weapon on an OHV from 1 h before sunrise to noon applies only in WMUs 400–446.
- Grizzly: the guide carries no grizzly season anywhere (no grizzly row in any season table).

CWD (p. 66): "For 2026: Mandatory requirement to submit DEER harvested in the following WMUs: … 300, 302, 303, 304, 305, 306, 308, 310, 312, 314, 316, 318, 320, 322, 324, 332, 334, 402, 404, 406, 412, 414 …" (within 30 days). 326 is not listed. "Since then we have tested over 124,000 heads and found CWD in 5,982 mule deer, 1,200 white-tailed deer, 78 elk and 29 moose."

## What it means for Clearwater
- [I] On the friend's days (Sun 18 and Mon 19 Oct 2026): white-tailed deer of either sex by rifle in 326 but by bow only in 324; elk (3-point bulls; antlerless too in 324) by bow only in both WMUs, rifle from 1 Nov; moose and rifle mule deer only with a drawn special licence; black bear open to 30 Nov; grouse open; no sharp-tailed grouse, no grizzly, cougar not until 1 Dec; wolves open without a licence.
- [I] The two WMUs split along the North Fork Road and Hwy 752 (regs-wmu-boundaries.md); crossing that road changes the deer weapon. The app's zone model (one zone per area) cannot say this today.
