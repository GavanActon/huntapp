## What it means for Clearwater
- [I] 324 and 326 yield 0.3–0.4 elk per 100 km² a year: 13–17× less than Bearberry (320) and 40–50× less than the Porcupine Hills (305). Over the 256 km² box that is about one elk a year.
- [I] Implied elk hunters (harvest ÷ success, crude because success is rounded): ~130–200 a season in each of 324 and 326 against ~500 in 320. Success of 1–7% sits below the Foothills-zone general average of 7.8%.
- [I] Range gate: this is the plan's "a season with near-zero harvest" case. A testable rule: a 5-year mean below 1 elk per 100 km² a year, with success under 5% or no elk on the latest winter survey, means "rare here". By that rule 324, 326 and 322 are rare, 318 is borderline, and 320, 332 and 305 are present and hunted.
- [I] Only archery (S1–O31) overlaps the rut here; the rifle season (N1–N30) is a post-rut, often snowy hunt.
- [I] Data: a box in WMU 320 could carry 1 m (HD) wind from open HRDEM; Clearwater, 324, 326 and 305 would need AltaLIS lidar or stay SD.
