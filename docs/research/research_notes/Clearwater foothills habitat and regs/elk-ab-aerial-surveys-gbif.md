## What it means for Clearwater
- [I] The three mid-winter surveys of the units the box sits in or touches (324 and 318 in Jan 2018, 326 in Jan 2020) found no elk, while the same design found herds in 320 (167) and 322 (57) in Dec 2018. Elk are in the region, not on these units in winter.
- [I] A 25% moose transect can miss clumped elk (groups of 46–125) and dense conifer hides them, so zero means "few", not "none". The harvest series (elk-ab-harvest-draws-2010-2025.md) agrees: a handful a year.
- [I] GBIF agrees: nothing within 25 km, though Hwy 752 and the gravel network run through the box (place-roads-alberta-access-layer.md). Use record counts only; most records are CC BY-NC.
- [I] The elk nearest the box are the farmland-edge herds to the east and north (322, 332, 336), the Bearberry–Sundre herds to the south-east (320) and the mountain and Ya Ha Tinda animals to the west and south-west (429, 418).
