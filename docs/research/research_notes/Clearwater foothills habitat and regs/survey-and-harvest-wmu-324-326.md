## Extracted facts
WMU 326 moose survey (22–23 Jan 2020, 28 east–west transects, 25% coverage):
- "WMU 326 is located south of the North Saskatchewan River, west of the Clearwater River, and east of the Ram River … The dominant land-cover is dense conifer forest making wildlife sighting challenging. Cut-blocks comprise about 35% of the unit."
- 35 moose in 22 groups; density **0.14/km²** (90% CI 0.07–0.20); estimate 141 (75–206). Earlier: 2005 RSB 300 (0.29/km²); 2010 RSB 417 (0.40/km²), bull:cow:calf 80:100:13. "the marked decline in moose populations is a concern."
- "No elk were observed." 16 feral horses and 11 deer (4 mule, 5 white-tailed, 2 unknown) were seen.
- Moose seasons then: archery 25 Aug–23 Sept, rifle 24 Sept–31 Oct and 1–30 Nov, all by special licence; "There is no antlerless moose season in WMU 326. The five-year average hunter success for antlered moose is approximately 35% (range 25-50%)."

WMU 324 (with 318) moose survey (4–6 Jan 2018):
- 324: 37 moose in 20 groups; density **0.15/km²** (90% CI 0.08–0.21); estimate 150 (86–215); bull:cow:calf 59:100:59. 2010 RSB: 195 (0.19/km²). WMU 318: 0.29/km² (337).
- "there were no elk observed during the 2018 survey." Deer too few for an estimate.
- Then both archery 1 Sept–31 Oct and rifle 1–30 Nov were special licence only; five-year special-licence success: antlered moose 37% and antlerless 26% in 324.

## What it means for Clearwater
- [I] Moose are thin (0.14–0.15/km² in mid-winter, about a third of 2010 in 326) and hunted only by a small draw. Relative heat is still useful, but the verdict should not promise moose.
- [I] Elk: two mid-winter surveys of these exact WMUs saw none, and the harvest is 2–11 a year at 1–7% success; implied hunter numbers (harvest ÷ success) are on the order of 100–200 elk hunters a year per WMU for a handful of elk. In the range gate this is "present, hunted, but rare": an edge population.
- [I] White-tailed deer are the game here: ~790 taken in 2025 across the two WMUs, at 34–46% success. Implied ~1,150 deer hunters in 324 and ~770 in 326 over the season.
- [I] Black bears are hunted mostly in 324 (bait is allowed in part of it).

## Not confirmed
- Moose harvest for 2021–2023 by WMU (not extracted). Harvest estimates come from a hunter survey; small numbers carry large error.
- Whether the 2020 decline in 326 is real or partly a change of survey method (the report itself says the methods differ).
