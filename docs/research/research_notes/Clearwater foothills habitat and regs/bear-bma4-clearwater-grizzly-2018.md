- **Evidence:** [S] for the inventory; [G] for the occurrence summaries
- **Where and when:** DNA hair-snag grid of 148 cells of 49 km² each (about 7,252 km²) across BMA 4, between Highway 1 and Highway 11. In the east it reached into "land minimally occupied or unoccupied by grizzly bears"; in the west, into Banff National Park. Elevation 800–3,500 m. Sampled in 2018 and compared with 2005. Occurrence records cover 2009–2020.

## Extracted facts
- **Bears detected:** 64 in 2018 (35 males, 29 females) vs 42 in 2005 (17 males, 25 females).
- **Population:** N = 88 (59–130) in 2018 vs 42 (32–50) in 2005, an increase of about 6% per year (5–9%). Density 9.64 per 1,000 km² (6.54–14.27) vs 4.39 (3.52–5.48).
- **2018 density by zone** (Table 7, per 1,000 km² of habitat area):
  - Core: 11.64 (SE 1.72). Females 6.02, males 5.62.
  - Secondary: 5.78 (SE 1.14). Females 3.22, males 2.56.
  - Banff: 20.78.
  - For comparison, 2005 Secondary densities were females 1.16 and males 0.84 (Table 9).
- **Distribution:**
  - "many detections occurred along the western side of the study area with a declining density gradient to the east".
  - The "largest relative increase occurring in secondary areas".
  - "female bears are spreading eastward over time while male bears continue to occupy similar areas".
  - Most home-range centres were in the Core zone; only 4 of 17 male and 1 of 25 female centres were in Secondary. These counts match the 2005 detections.
- **Eastern edge:** "it is widely recognized that there are grizzly bears which are seen to the east of the sampling grid … in the agricultural zone". Two bears were GPS-collared there in 2019.
- **Black bears:** 40% of species-identified hair samples and 75% of scat samples were black bear. The FAQ suggests black bears "messed up" some hair snags before grizzlies arrived.
- **Occurrence summary seasons:** "Pre Berry (den emergence to July 15), Berry (July 16 to September 15) and Post Berry (September 16 to den up)."
- **Occurrences by season:**
  - 2019: 81 records (the 2020 report gives 89 for 2019). 58% pre-berry, 30% berry, 12% post-berry.
  - 2020: 39 records. 64%, 28%, 8%.
- **Attractants:**
  - 2019: livestock 20%, natural vegetation 17%, grain 13%, garbage 13%, beehives 10%.
  - 2020: natural vegetation 28%, grain 14%, livestock carcasses 11%, livestock 8%.
  - "Grain continues to be an ongoing concern" (faulty bins, wooden hopper bottoms).
- **Deaths and translocations:**
  - 21 known human-caused grizzly deaths in BMA 4, 2009–2020. "The illegal killing of grizzly bears is the primary cause of mortality" (7 cases).
  - Of 16 deaths with a known zone (2014–2019): 8 in the Support Zone and 7 in the Recovery Zone.
  - 23 translocations in 2009–2020; 43% were for attacks on livestock.
- **Study area text:** "Moderate to high levels of recreation, including … off-highway vehicle use and hunting". "There have been multiple human fatalities and serious injuries to people from bears in BMA 4 over the years." Conflicts include "damage to stored grain and bears feeding on livestock carcasses near developments". The 2005 inventory found "Densities were highest in the western, more remote portions of the BMA."

## What it means for Clearwater
- [I] **Grizzlies in the box.** At about 5.8 per 1,000 km² over the Secondary part (77%, about 197 km²) and less over the Support part, the box holds about one grizzly home-range centre. Several bears' ranges probably overlap it. Grizzlies are few, but numbers are rising about 6% a year and females are moving east.
- [I] **Where conflicts happen.** Fall conflict risk in this BMA concentrates at the farm edge (grain, livestock carcasses, beehives). Hunter–bear encounters do not show up as a category in these summaries.
- [I] **Date.** Alberta's own BMA 4 berry→post-berry boundary (15/16 September) is a defensible date for the grizzly fall switch here.

## Not confirmed
- Whether the box lies inside the 2018 grid. By its coordinates it sits just south of Highway 11, probably near the north-east of the grid.
- Zone confidence intervals in Table 7 came out garbled in the PDF text.
- Occurrence summaries for 2021 onward were not read.
- The 2005 estimate is given variously as 41–47 depending on the analysis.
