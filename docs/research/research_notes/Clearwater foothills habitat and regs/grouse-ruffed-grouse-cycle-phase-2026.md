# Ruffed grouse cycle phase: Minnesota's 2026 survey and the Alberta gap (2026)
- **Source:** Roy, C. 2026. 2026 Minnesota Ruffed Grouse Survey. Minnesota DNR, Forest Wildlife Populations and Research Group, Grand Rapids (4 June 2026); https://files.dnr.state.mn.us/wildlife/grouse/reports/ruffed/rgrouse-survey-2026.pdf . Alberta context: Scammell, B. "Slim pickings in the wilds." *Red Deer Advocate*, 15 October 2010; https://www.reddeeradvocate.com/columns/slim-pickings-in-the-wilds/
- **Read:** 2026-10-17 (MN report: summary, introduction, methods and results text; Advocate column: full text)
- **Evidence:** [S] agency science for Minnesota; [H] press for the Alberta column.
- **Where and when:** Minnesota spring drumming routes (10 stops each; 129 routes in 2026). Central Alberta grouse country in fall 2010.

## Extracted facts
- MN 2026 statewide: "Mean ruffed grouse drums per stop (dps) were 1.7 statewide (95% confidence interval = 1.5 – 2.0), which is down from last year and consistent with the peak in the population cycle occurring in 2024."
- MN 2026 by region (dps): Northeast 1.9 (n = 103 routes), Northwest 2.4 (n = 5), Central Hardwoods 1.0 (n = 13), Southeast 0.3 (n = 8). Three regions "are clearly in the declining phase of the cycle". The Northwest is the exception, rising, but only 5 of 8 routes were run.
- MN cycle: it "cycles on average every 10 years, but peaks in the cycle vary from 8 to 11 years apart". "The last 2 peaks occurred during 2009 (2.1 dps) and 2017 (2.1 dps), with lows occurring during 2005 (0.8 dps) and 2013 (0.9 dps)." The 2024 peak was "a little earlier than expected."
- MN: the spring index tracked fall harvest before the early 2000s, but "in recent decades this relationship has weakened".
- Alberta, 2010 column: hunters reported scarce ruffed grouse, and the debate was whether this was "the low of the notorious 10-year grouse cycle" or nesting failure after "two cold, wet springs".
- Alberta, other sources (see their notes): FRI 1999 calls Alberta ruffed grouse "cyclic with major fluctuations every 8-10 years". In ACA 2026, half of the trappers who think grouse cycle put it at 4–7 years. ACA has no forest grouse forecast.

## What it means for Clearwater
- [I] No Alberta source found states the 2025–2026 phase.
- [I] Minnesota's phase may not transfer. The 2010 central Alberta "low" came one year after a Minnesota peak (2009), so the two regions were probably out of phase then, unless the 2010 scarcity was weather-driven.
- [I] Don't apply a cycle multiplier to habitat scores. Show "cycle phase unknown in Alberta" and let the party's flush counts in the hunt log set expectations.

## Not confirmed
- Alberta's ruffed grouse cycle phase in 2025–2026.
- Whether the Minnesota and Alberta cycles are in step.
- The peak and low years from the Rochester, Alberta series (Keith, Rusch et al.) were not obtained.
- The Minnesota 2025 values (statewide 1.8 dps vs 2.3 in 2024) were seen only in a search-result summary; the 2025 report was not opened.
