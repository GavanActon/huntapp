## Extracted facts
- Inside the box: ruffed grouse 4 records (one at Prairie Creek PRA on 2024-10-06, 1,164 m); spruce grouse 3; dusky 0; sharp-tailed 0; ptarmigan 0.
- Within 30 km of the box centre:
  - Ruffed grouse 149 records (Sept 17, Oct 14, Nov 14).
  - Spruce grouse 24 (Sept 6, Oct 3, Nov 4), e.g. "Strachan Area" 1993-09-27 at 1,095 m on the box's east edge.
  - Dusky grouse 1: Baseline Mountain, 2024-12-29, 1,902 m, 20 km from centre.
  - Sharp-tailed grouse 0.
  - White-tailed ptarmigan 2: 1924 specimens labelled "Rocky Mt. Park" but georeferenced near Rocky Mountain House at 1,006 m. Probably a georeferencing error ("Rocky Mountains Park" was Banff's old name).
- Dusky grouse in the wide box: 9 records ever, all west of 115.43 W.
  - Sept 2009 at 1,483 m; Sept 2021 near Ram Falls at 1,616 m.
  - Nordegg in June 2022 at 1,330 m; Colosseum Mountain in May 2023; Ram Falls PP in Aug 2022.
  - Two Provincial Museum records from 1987–88 in the Ram/Hummingbird headwaters.
- Dusky grouse east of 115.45 W (51.6–53.0 N): only 4 records ever. Three are 1896–97 specimens labelled "70 miles NW of Calgary" with 48–113 km coordinate uncertainty; the fourth is the December Baseline Mountain bird.

  The 3 October records at ≤1,400 m are in the Bow Valley/Canmore at 1,300–1,323 m, mountain valleys rather than foothills. The low September records (1,040–1,241 m) are in the Jasper, Hinton (Switzer PP) and Grande Cache valleys.
- White-tailed ptarmigan in the wide box: 10 records, in April (the 1924 specimens), May and July only.
  - Alpine points: 2,081 m (2003), 2,163 m (1987, a peak near Hummingbird Creek) and 2,294 m (Ranger Creek headwaters, 1987).
  - One May 2022 record sits on the Nordegg place-name point (1,372 m), probably a centroid rather than the bird's location.
  - Willow and rock ptarmigan: 0.
- Sharp-tailed grouse in the wide box: 3 records. Goldeye Lake PRA 2020-10-23 at 1,376 m (west, near Nordegg); north of Sundre on Hwy 22, 2017-10-25 at 1,102 m; a Sundre specimen from 1950 at 823 m.
- Cade & Hoffman 1993 abstract (13 males and 19 females radio-tagged, north-central Colorado):
  - Males moved a median 488 m upslope (183–671 m) and 10.5 km (1.0–29.4 km) to winter areas.
  - Females moved a median 122 m (−61 to 760 m) and 1.0 km (0.1–28.0 km).
  - Both sexes reached winter areas "around mid-to-late October".
- FRI guide: "During the winter, this species migrates to higher elevations (up to >3,600 m) to conifer-dominated stands including Douglas fir, subalpine fir, lodgepole pine, limber pine, Engelmann spruce, and western hemlock." Alberta status: Secure.
- Stelfox 1988 (separate note): near Hinton, blue grouse summered only in a clearcut near the mountains, never in blocks more than 15 km out, and were never seen in winter.

## What it means for Clearwater
- [I] Dusky (blue) grouse: treat as absent from the box in October and score 0. Here it is a Front Range bird. Fall records sit at about 1,850–2,050 m, and the nearest record of any season is a December bird on the Baseline Mountain ridge at 1,902 m, above the box's 1,372 m top. The open season in WMUs 324/326 is a legal opportunity, not evidence of presence.
- [I] Ptarmigan: absent; score 0. There is no alpine in the box, and regional records are at about 2,100–2,300 m in the Front Ranges, none in fall.
- [I] Sharp-tailed grouse: no records within 30 km, and no season. A layer is not needed, but an ID warning is (see the ACA note).
- [I] Spruce grouse are present in fall around the box (Strachan, the Prairie Creek country), but at roughly 1 record per 6 ruffed grouse records.

## Not confirmed
- The records are opportunistic and cluster on roads, parks and campgrounds. Ridge-top winter range is under-sampled, and a lack of records does not prove absence. No systematic survey (BBS, ABMI ARU) for dusky grouse in Clearwater County was checked.
- eBird hotspot coordinates can be several km off. A CDEM elevation at the record point is not necessarily the bird's elevation.
- The Alberta Breeding Bird Atlas (2007) dusky grouse map was not checked. The full text of Cade & Hoffman was not read.
