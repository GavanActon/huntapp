## Extracted facts
**Zone and drainage**
- The Eastern Slopes units are "the Oldman and Bow rivers (ES1), the Red Deer and North Saskatchewan rivers (ES2), the Athabasca and Pembina rivers (ES3) and the Smoky River (ES4)". ES2 covers "the North Saskatchewan River watershed upstream of Highway 22/39 near Drayton Valley."
- Prairie Creek ends on the Clearwater River: in OSM its last node lies on the Clearwater River line at 52.3302 N, 114.9215 W, about 4.0 km above the North Saskatchewan at Rocky Mountain House. NRCan places Prairie Creek at 39-7-W5.
- North Prairie Creek joins Prairie Creek at 52.2698 N, 115.1554 W, and Lick Creek at 52.2517 N, 115.2984 W; both junctions are inside the box. Vetch Creek (52.2125 N, 115.0817 W) and Swan Creek (52.1944 N, 115.0131 W) join east of the box.
- The OSM "North Fork Road" crosses Prairie Creek inside the box at about 52.261 N, 115.169 W, then runs NW through it.

**Prairie Creek rules (ES2 rivers table, 2026)**
- Mainstem upstream of the North Fork Road and all tributaries, except Swan Creek and tributaries between Vetch and Swan Creek:
  - OPEN APR. 1 TO AUG. 31: bait ban; pike 3; mountain whitefish 5 over 30 cm; 2 trout.
  - OPEN SEPT. 1 TO OCT. 31: bait ban; pike 3; whitefish 0; 0 trout.
- Mainstem and tributaries between Vetch and Swan Creeks: OPEN APR. 1 TO AUG. 31; bait ban; pike 3; whitefish 0; 0 trout.
- "The remainder of the mainstem downstream of the North Fork Road": OPEN APR. 1 TO OCT. 31; bait ban; walleye 3 over 50 cm; pike 3; whitefish 0; 0 trout.
- "If a listed waterbody does not have a season listed, it is CLOSED to fishing during that period." Prairie Creek is therefore closed 1 Nov – 31 Mar.
- Table legend: "An empty cell indicates the species is not likely present at that waterbody". Prairie Creek's sauger, burbot, goldeye, rainbow, cutthroat and brown trout cells are empty. The ES2 rivers table has no brook trout column, so brook trout fall under the trout total.

**ES2 defaults and province-wide rules**
- Streams:
  - OPEN Apr 1–Aug 31: bull trout 0; Arctic grayling 0; trout 2; cutthroat over 35 cm; whitefish 5 over 30 cm; walleye 3 over 50 cm; pike 3; perch 15; burbot 10; bait ban.
  - OPEN Sept 1–Oct 31: "Trout and Mountain Whitefish limit 0".
  - CLOSED Nov 1–Mar 31.
- "Fishing with bait in ES2 streams is not allowed." Angling through ice into flowing water or beaver ponds is not permitted in ES2.
- Default for lakes: open all year; bull trout 0; trout 5; bait allowed, but not bait fish.
- Province-wide maximum possession: "Trout – 5 in total, combined of: 0 bull trout (native to Alberta) ... 5 rainbow trout; 5 brown trout; 5 brook trout; 5 tiger trout."

**Lakes**
- Rat Lake (SW11-38-9-W5; 52.247 N, 115.184 W, inside the box) is on the ES2 put-and-take list. The provincial put-and-take rules are: "OPEN all year; Bait is allowed; 5 trout of any size; 3 Northern Pike of any size; 15 Yellow Perch of any size".
- Rat Lake stocking in 2026:
  - Brook trout (Beitty Resort strain, triploid 3N): 2,500 at 4.9 cm average, 21 May 2026.
  - Tiger trout (Beitty/Bow River, 3N): 700 at 27.4 cm, 1 October 2026.
- Cow Lake (NE12-38-8-W5, about 8 km east of the box) is in the ES2 lakes table: OPEN MAY 15 TO MAR. 31; bait and bait fish allowed; walleye 3; pike 3; perch 15; 5 trout.
- Cow Lake stocking in 2026: 24,000 rainbow trout. Two lots of 8,000 Trout Lodge/Kamloops AF3N at 15.1 cm (6 and 7 May) and 8,000 Riverence AF2N at 20.5 cm (15 May).

**Species records (GBIF)**
- University of Alberta Museums, 8–9 August 1965, Prairie Creek inside the box (52.263 N, 115.173 W and 52.307 N, 115.234 W) and upstream (52.189 N, 115.369 W): brook trout, mountain whitefish, longnose sucker, burbot and longnose dace.
- Same collection: Vetch Creek 1965, brook trout; Swan Creek, longnose dace.
- iNaturalist:
  - Brown trout, two records in 2018, on lower Prairie Creek about 15 km downstream (east) of the box (52.229–52.230 N, 114.937–114.949 W; 0.01–0.06 km from the creek line, 4.4–4.7 km from the Clearwater).
  - Mountain whitefish in 2024 near the Prairie Creek mouth.
  - Brook trout on 2025-06-22 at Rat Lake (stocked).
  - Pike in 2022 at Cow Lake.
- Bull trout records (museum 1965–1987; iNaturalist 2023–2025) are all in the upper Clearwater system or the North Saskatchewan at Rocky. The upper Clearwater sites are the Clearwater River at 51.99 N and Seven Mile, Rocky, Elk and Peppers creeks. None are on Prairie Creek.
- No Arctic grayling or cutthroat records exist in the Prairie Creek area. The only nearby rainbow trout record (2017) is near the Clearwater–North Saskatchewan confluence.

## What it means for Clearwater
- [I] Fish in the box's creeks:
  - Brook trout, introduced, are the trout here.
  - Mountain whitefish, with longnose sucker, longnose dace and burbot.
  - Pike are possible in lower reaches and ponds (the regs set a pike limit).
  - Brown trout live in lower Prairie Creek well east of the box. Whether they reach the box is not shown.
  - Bull trout: none recorded in Prairie Creek; strays from the Clearwater are possible and must be released.
  - Athabasca rainbow trout belong to the Athabasca basin (ES3), not this one.
- [I] For the hunt dates (18–19 October 2026): all Prairie Creek water in the box, including North Prairie and Lick creeks as tributaries, is catch-and-release for trout and whitefish, bait ban, and closes 31 October.
- [I] Rat Lake is the place in the box to keep fish: open all year, bait allowed, 5 trout. 700 catchable tiger trout went in on 1 October 2026.
