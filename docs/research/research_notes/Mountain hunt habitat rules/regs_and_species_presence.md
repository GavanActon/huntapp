## 1. Blanchard River, BC: which MU, and the range-gate table for each species

### Takeaway
The site (59.9673, -136.7204) falls in **MU 6-28** (Skeena Region, GMZ 6f). It also lies inside the **Haines Highway Area Moose Restricted Area** (Map F39).

For the week of 12 Oct 2026, these are open to a resident:
- moose: spike-fork, tripalm or 10-point bulls only;
- mountain goat: until Oct 15;
- black bear, wolf, grouse (ruffed, spruce, sooty/dusky) and ptarmigan.

Thinhorn sheep is **LEH only** ("Tatshenshini" draw). These have **no open season** in 6-28:
- caribou;
- grizzly;
- mule deer, white-tailed deer and elk;
- sharp-tailed grouse.

### Inferences
- **Moose.** Presence is inferred because the ministry drew a dedicated restricted area for the corridor (Map F39). I found no survey figure.
- **Sheep.** Presence in 6-28/6-29 is inferred from two "Tatshenshini" sheep LEH hunts with 9 and 14 authorizations. BC uses "thinhorn". I did not confirm whether these are Dall's or Stone's type. Dall's would be expected in the far northwest, but this is **unconfirmed**.
- **Goat.** For the app, goat is "present, hunted" at Blanchard River. The goat LEH zone in 6-28 (Tagish Highlands, Zone B) is at White Pass/Bennett, about 100 km east, so it does not apply at the Haines Highway site.
- **Caribou.** Gate it as "no season". Presence is unknown, so either "absent" or "edge" is defensible. No Skeena caribou LEH zone touches 6-28.

### Gaps
- No survey or occupancy data was found for moose, goat, sheep, black bear or ptarmigan in 6-28. Presence is inferred from the regulation structure and BC Parks wording.
- Whether mule deer, sharp-tailed grouse or dusky grouse occur along the Haines Highway corridor was not confirmed.
- The 2026 Skeena sheep LEH was drawn in February 2026, under the earlier LEH synopsis. The codes 6043/6044 and "Aug 1-Oct 15" are printed in the 2026-2027 LEH synopsis under the "2027 Early Skeena Mountain Sheep Draw". I assume the 2026 hunt used the same dates; this is not verified.
- I could not parse the corrections/updates PDF (the 2024–2026 file returned 0 pages). Check www.gov.bc.ca/hunting for any in-season change before the hunt.

## 2. BC key questions: moose antler rules, caribou, sheep, goat, grizzly, park, highway corridor

### Takeaway
- **Moose:** in the Haines Highway area of 6-28/6-29 it is any bull Aug 23–31, then spike-fork, tripalm or 10-point bulls only, Sept 1 to Oct 31.
- **Caribou:** closed (no LEH here).
- **Sheep:** LEH only.
- **Goat:** general season Aug 1 to Oct 15.
- **Grizzly:** the 2017 closure still holds in the 2026–2028 synopsis.
- **Park:** hunting is allowed in Tatshenshini-Alsek Park during open seasons.
- **Highway:** there is no special Haines Highway no-hunting zone. The province-wide 15 m highway road-allowance ban applies, plus a 400 m ban near highways *inside* parks.

### Inferences
- **First Nation.** The Blanchard River and Haines Highway corridor are most likely **Champagne and Aishihik First Nations** traditional territory, since the adjoining park and the Tatshenshini watershed are CAFN's. I found no source that puts the site in Taku River Tlingit territory; TRT territory centres on Atlin and the Taku. Treat "TRT" as **unconfirmed**. Overlap with Carcross/Tagish First Nation's asserted area was not checked.
- **Park buffer.** If the park boundary comes within 400 m of the Haines Highway centre line, any park land inside that strip is closed to hunting and shooting. Elsewhere the park is open in season. I did not measure the boundary-to-road distance.

### Gaps
- Exact park boundary geometry relative to the highway at 59.967 N was not checked. A BC Parks or TANTALIS WFS query would settle it.
- I did not find a separate CAFN or BC notice to hunters for MU 6-28; none appears in the Region 6 First Nations notices, which name Lake Babine, Teslin Tlingit, Kaska, Gitxsan and Haida.

## 3. Highland Lake (Et'aghro), Yukon, GMS 4-09: the range-gate table for each species

### Takeaway
In GMS 4-09 the following are open to licensed hunters:
- male moose, Aug 1 to Oct 31: a general season, **not** part of the Mayo threshold hunt, which covers 4-04 to 4-06;
- male caribou, Aug 1 to Oct 31: the **Ethel Lake herd**, which is legally open, but the government, the Mayo RRC and the First Nation of Na-Cho Nyäk Dun ask hunters not to take it;
- male sheep, Aug 1 to Oct 31, full curl or 8 years and older;
- grizzly and black bear, spring and fall;
- wolf, grouse (including sharp-tailed) and ptarmigan.

Goat is **CLOSED** in all of Zone 4. Deer are **PHA lottery only**. Elk fall under the **elk exclusion area** permit, open to any resident.

## 4. Yukon key questions: permit hunts near Mayo, closed subzones, settlement land, non-residents

### Takeaway
- **Near Mayo:**
  - the **Mayo moose threshold hunt** (GMS 2-56, 2-58, 2-59, 2-62, 2-63, 4-04 to 4-06) runs Sept 1 to Oct 31 or until the threshold, with special guiding barred;
  - **Ddhaw Ghro (4-03)** is closed to all hunting;
  - **4-51** is closed to all big game.

  4-09 is none of these.
- **First Nation land:** written consent is needed for any hunting on Category A Settlement Land and fee-simple land, and for elk or bison on Category A or B. Undeveloped Category B land is open without permission for other species.
- **Non-residents:** they need a registered outfitter or a special guide for big game. Special guide licences cannot be used for sheep, goat, elk, deer, grizzly or wolverine.

### Cited Findings
- **Mayo threshold hunt.** "There is a threshold hunt for moose in place for the Mayo area (GMS 4–04 to 4–06)… Special guiding non-residents for moose in these subzones is prohibited." The moose section's full list: "Game Management Subzones: 2–56, 2–58, 2–59, 2–62, 2–63 and 4–04 to 4–06", "Dates: September 1 to October 31 or when the threshold limit is reached". A kill must be reported within 72 hours. Threshold hotline: 1-833-560-HUNT (4868). [G] [Yukon 2026-27 p. 36–37, 50](https://emrlibrary.gov.yk.ca/emrlibrary/environment/yukon-hunting-regulations-summary/2026-27.pdf)
- **Closed areas.** "Ddhaw Ghro (GMS 4–03) is closed to all hunting." "Game Management Subzone 4–51 is closed to all big game hunting." [G] same, p. 50
- **Ethel Lake herd.** "The Government of Yukon asks all hunters not to hunt the Ethel Lake caribou herd (GMS 4–01 to 4–04, 4–09, 4–10). This herd is open to hunting, but cannot sustain a large harvest. The Mayo District Renewable Resources Council has recommended all hunters avoid harvesting from this herd until calf survival improves. The First Nation of Na-Cho Nyäk Dun and the Selkirk First Nation are asking their citizens to refrain from hunting this herd as well." [G] same, p. 50
- **Caribou bag.** "Your first caribou can be from any herd. If you want to harvest a second caribou, it has to be from the Porcupine caribou herd." Caribou harvest must be reported "no later than 15 days after the end of the month". [G] same, p. 29
- **Settlement land, consent.** "First Nations require you obtain their written permission (consent): before hunting any big game or small game species on all Category A Settlement Lands and fee simple lands; and before hunting wood bison and elk on Category A and B Settlement Lands. Verbal permission is not consent." [G] same, p. 13
- **Settlement land, access.** "You may hunt on undeveloped Category B Settlement Lands without written permission from the local First Nation." "You may cross Category A and Category B Settlement Lands to access crown land to hunt…" On navigable water bordering Settlement Land, you may hunt wildlife standing on gravel bars, the shoreline below the ordinary high water mark, or islands that are not Settlement Land. Hunters "must comply with any laws enacted by the First Nation for managing the land and resources." [G] same, p. 14–15
- **Contacts.** The First Nation of Na-Cho Nyäk Dun (Mayo) is listed at 867-996-2265. The Mayo conservation officer is at 867-996-2202. [G] same, contacts
- **Non-residents.** "Non-residents can only hunt big game in the Yukon with: a registered Yukon outfitter; or a Yukon resident with a special guide licence."
  - "Special guide licences are limited to moose, caribou, wolf, coyote, black bear and wood bison… Goat, sheep, elk, deer, grizzly bear and wolverine cannot be hunted under a special guide licence."
  - Special guiding for moose is barred in 4-03 to 4-06, 4-44 to 4-46 and 4-51, among others. For caribou it is barred in 4-03 and 4-51.
  - "Non-residents can buy a small game licence and hunt small game and game birds without a guide."
  - Only Yukon residents can apply for PHAs. [G] same, p. 6, 10–11
  - Inference: non-residents hunt sheep and grizzly only through a registered outfitter, here outfitting concession 7.
- **Roads.** "It's against the law to discharge a firearm on or across the travelled portion of a public road or highway. This includes the shoulder." **Off-road vehicles** are restricted in "alpine areas 1,400 metres above sea level or higher", in Ddhaw Ghro and elsewhere. [G] same, p. 17–24
- **2026-27 changes.** These cover only residency documentation and one Kluane Wildlife Sanctuary sheep permit. Nothing affects Zone 4. [G] same, p. 2; [Yukon news release](https://yukon.ca/en/news/government-yukon-shares-updates-2026-27-hunting-and-fishing-licensing-year)

### Inferences
- **Settlement land.** If the Highland Lake site is not on Settlement Land, as the brief says, licensed hunting needs no First Nation consent. Even so, the app should flag the Ethel Lake herd request and the NND ask as "please don't" for caribou in 4-09.
- **Range gate.** Model caribou in 4-09 as "present, hunted, voluntary restraint", a fifth state beyond the four in the brief. The same pattern applies to threshold hunts, which can close in season, for example moose in 4-04 to 4-06.

### Gaps
- No NND-specific hunting by-law or notice was found online for licensed hunters, beyond the summary's general settlement land rules.
- Outfitting concession 7's operator and boundaries were not researched.

## 5. Deer and elk at both sites

### Takeaway
- **BC 6-28:** no deer or elk season at all.
- **Yukon 4-09:** deer by PHA lottery only; elk by the exclusion-area permit, whose purpose is to keep elk out of non-core range.

Mule deer have spread north through the Yukon as far as Dawson City, so they are possible but sparse near Mayo. White-tailed deer and elk are probably absent at both sites.

### Cited Findings
- **BC deer and elk.** Skeena mule deer seasons cover MUs 6-1 to 6-15 and 6-30 only. White-tailed deer cover "6-1 to 6-11, 6-14, 6-15, 6-30" only. Elk cover "6-1, 6-2, 6-4 to 6-6, 6-8, 6-9" and "6-13" only. MU 6-28 has none. [G] [BC synopsis p. 50](https://www2.gov.bc.ca/assets/gov/sports-recreation-arts-and-culture/outdoor-recreation/fishing-and-hunting/hunting/regulations/hunting-trapping-synopsis-region-6-skeena.pdf)
- **Yukon deer.** "Female deer are protected from hunting… You need a permit to hunt deer. Apply for a permit through the Permit Hunt Authorization (PHA) lottery." Head and hide must be submitted (winter tick and CWD checks). [G] [Yukon 2026-27 p. 32](https://emrlibrary.gov.yk.ca/emrlibrary/environment/yukon-hunting-regulations-summary/2026-27.pdf)
- **Yukon elk.**
  - "Hunting in the elk buffer areas (Braeburn and Takhini herds) is by Permit Hunt Authorization lottery only… The Braeburn elk herd Permit Hunt Authorization is not offered this season."
  - "The elk exclusion area includes all the Game Management Zones except: GMZ 10; GMZ 11; the elk ranges (core and buffer area); GMS 4–03; and GMS 4–51… Any Yukon resident hunter can get a permit to hunt elk in the elk exclusion area from April 1 to March 31… You will get this permit for free when you purchase an elk seal."
  - GMS 4-09 is therefore inside the exclusion area. [G] same, p. 33–34
- **Mule deer range.** Mule deer arrived in the Yukon from BC around 1900 and now range "from the BC/Yukon border right through to Dawson City". A department director put them at "500 to 800 animals". [S] (search snippet) [Yukon News, "Shoot 'em first, count 'em later"](https://www.yukon-news.com/news/shoot-em-first-count-em-later/)

### Inferences
- **Mule deer (both sites):** gate as "edge of range, no general season". BC: no season. Yukon: PHA only.
- **Elk:** gate as "absent". Yukon 4-09 offers an exclusion-area permit, but heat scoring for elk there would be meaningless.
- **White-tailed deer:** gate as "absent" at both.

### Gaps
- No primary Yukon map of deer distribution near Mayo was found.
- The Yukon News figure is undated in the snippet; the article was not fetched.
- No source on deer along the Haines Highway was found.
